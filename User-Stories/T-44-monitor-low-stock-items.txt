Title: Monitor Low-Stock Items

User Story:
As an Inventory Manager,
I want to monitor low-stock items,
so that products can be restocked on time.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. System monitors product stock quantities.
2. Low-stock products are identified.
3. Low-stock items are displayed to the Inventory Manager.
4. A low-stock notification appears when stock reaches the defined level.
5. Inventory Manager can identify products that need restocking.
